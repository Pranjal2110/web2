Problem Statement & Proposed Solution

⚠️ Current Challenges in Libraries
  • Inventory Discrepancies: Traditional library ledgers suffer from manual counting errors, lost inventory, and lack of real-time stock sync.
  • No Strict Checkout Guardrails: Patrons frequently borrow out-of-stock books, hoard multiple copies of high-demand titles, or exceed member borrowing quotas.
  • Uncollected Overdue Fines: Calculating fines manually based on calendar days is tedious, error-prone, and causes financial loss for public institutions.
  • Lack of Operational Telemetry: Administrators lack real-time visibility into popular disciplines, stock turnover, and patron borrowing patterns.
💡 Our Solution: Athenaeum
  • Full-Stack SSR Platform: Built on Node.js, Express, EJS, and MongoDB Atlas to provide instant, secure, server-side rendered pages.
  • Multi-Layer Safety Guardrails: Automated algorithms reject 0-copy issues, enforce 3-book member limits, and eliminate duplicate loans.
  • Dynamic Overdue Fine Engine: Computes late penalties ($0.75/day) in real time, with a Time-Machine simulator (+3d, +7d, +15d) for instant verification.
  • Real-Time Analytics Telemetry: Live dashboard with physical copy utilization %, ranked popularity meters, and audit trails.

Features & Safety Guardrails

💡

Core Features & Strict Business Logic Guardrails

📋 Operational Features
  • Role-Based Portals: Dedicated Librarian workspace (desk, inventory, dossiers) and Member personal portal ("My Loans").
  • Librarian Book CRUD: Manage title, author, ISBN, category, copy counts, shelf location, and year.
  • Multi-Filter Search: Real-time keyword search, discipline categorization, and availability filtering.
  • Automated Copy Sync: Automatically decrements availableCopies on issue, and auto-increments upon book return.
🛡️ The 3 Mandatory Safety Guardrails
  • 1. Zero Available Copies Guard: Completely blocks checkout when availableCopies === 0 (e.g. Clean Code). Displays explicit out-of-stock badge.
  • 2. Per-Member Borrowing Quota: Strictly enforces patron limit (default 3 books). Blocks 4th borrow attempt until previous titles are returned.
  • 3. Duplicate Checkout Prevention: Rejects issuing a second physical copy of the same title to the same patron while an active checkout exists.
  • 4. Inventory Delete Protection: Librarians are blocked from deleting books with active checkouts.

Stretch Goal & Innovation: Automated Fine Engine & Telemetry

💰 Automated Overdue Fine Engine
  • Dynamic Calculation: Computes late penalties in real-time at $0.75 / day overdue against the simulated clock.
  • Time Machine Simulation: Header controls fast-forward time (+3d, +7d, +15d) to immediately verify overdue transitions and fines.
  • Fine Settlement Workflow: Supports cash collection (recording payment in patron ledger) and librarian staff waivers.
  • Visual Alerts: Red badges and warning banners alert patrons and staff to overdue books.
📊 Real-Time Analytics & Telemetry
  • High-Level KPIs: Real-time counters for Total Titles, Physical Copies, Active Loans, Overdue Books, and Fines.
  • Most-Borrowed Rankings: Dynamic popularity leaderboard tracking high-demand books.
  • Discipline Distribution: Proportional visual meters across Computer Science, Literature, Science, and Economics.
  • Audit Activity Trail: Chronological record of all book issues, returns, inventory edits, and waivers.
